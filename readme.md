# Amnios
Amnios is a puzzle game and interactive narrative exploring maternity as a vector of resilience within migratory journeys. Join three women in distinct geographical and political contexts: Nina, who crosses the Darién Gap while pregnant in search of a better life; Amani, who travels from the Democratic Republic of Congo to the Nakivale camps in Uganda to give birth; and Tania, who seeks her place in Geneva while navigating her new responsibilities as a mother and her Ukrainian cultural heritage.
